Hidden-tab spike

Krunk 0.10.0 (P3): what a tab keeps while it is hidden. Every 5 s the page reports its visibility, frames and timer ticks per second to /m, plus what its case checks: state (the text typed in the field and the scroll position), audio (the playing position; p plays), rtc (frames a local WebRTC call received), notify (n: a notification 8 s later) and dl (d: a slow 20-MB download).
