Busy tab

Krunk 0.10.0 (P1): a page that keeps a browser busy the way news, video and app pages do — an animation loop on a canvas, four 16-ms timers, a CSS animation, a muted looping video and a cross-site frame doing the same (the video only with ?video=1). Every 5 s it reports to /m how many animation frames and timer ticks it got per second, and whether the browser treats it as visible. A hidden (background) tab should get ~0 frames and ~1 tick/s.
