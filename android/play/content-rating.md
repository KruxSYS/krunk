# Content rating (IARC questionnaire): notes for Krunk for Android

Draft for the operator; the questionnaire is filled in Play Console › App content › Content
ratings. The wording of IARC's questions changes from time to time; the answers below follow
what the app does and should be mapped onto the questions as shown.

## Category

**"All other app types"** (not a game, not social/communication-only, not a reference/news
app). Krunk is a general-purpose web browser.

## Answers

| Topic | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Violence, blood, gore | No | The app itself has none. Krunk's own content is its screens, a new-tab page with bundled wallpapers, and its error and blocked-page screens. |
| Sexual content, nudity | No | Same. |
| Bad language, crude humour | No | Same. |
| Drugs, alcohol, tobacco | No | Same. |
| Gambling (real or simulated) | No | Same. |
| Fear, horror | No | Same. |
| **Does the app give users unrestricted access to the internet** (web browser, search engine) | **Yes** | It is a web browser: any address, any site, search engines. |
| Do users interact with or exchange content with other users (chat, posts, sharing)? | **No** for the app itself | Krunk has no chat, accounts, profiles or feeds of its own. Websites opened in it may have such features; that is covered by the "unrestricted internet" answer. The share sheet sends a link to another app the user picks. |
| Does the app share the user's current physical location with other users? | **No** | Location is passed only to a website the user allowed, after Krunk's and Android's prompts; never to other users by the app. If the question is read as "can location reach third parties", note it under unrestricted internet access, not here. |
| Can users buy digital goods in the app? | **No** | No in-app purchases or payments of its own. |
| Does the app contain ads? | No (separate declaration in App content) | |
| Is the app a "web browser or search engine" (where IARC asks it) | **Yes** | |

## Expected result

IARC derives the ratings per region. A browser normally gets a low content rating with an
interactive-element notice such as "Unrestricted Internet" (wording varies by rating board).
The exact age ratings were not checked; the questionnaire result decides.

## Related declarations (separate forms)

- **Target audience and content:** choose adult age groups only (18 and over, or 13+ at the
  lowest) and answer "No" to "appeals to children". Selecting any group under 13 brings the
  Families policy, which a browser with unrestricted internet access does not meet.
- **News app:** No. **Government app:** No.
