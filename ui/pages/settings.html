DNS

Name resolution

Who turns names into addresses. Custom: Krunk's own list, in order, over the engine's secure DNS-over-HTTPS client; Windows' resolver is never asked (a resolver with a name and no address is looked up once through it).

Resolvers

The first one answers; the next is asked after 2 seconds or an error. UDP, TCP, DNS over TLS, HTTPS (HTTP/2 and HTTP/3), QUIC, or an sdns:// stamp. DNSCrypt stamps are kept but skipped.

VPN

Only Krunk's connections

The VPN carries Krunk's connections only: normal and private windows, and Krunk's own updates, sync, filter lists and extensions. Other apps on this computer are not affected and nothing is installed in Windows. While the VPN is on but not connected, Krunk's connections stop: nothing goes around it.

Next to the shield: the VPN's state, with Connect and Disconnect.

Profiles

WireGuard (.conf) or OpenVPN (.ovpn) files from your VPN provider. They are stored encrypted for your Windows account; scripts in them never run.

    Your local network skips the VPN, so your router, printers and home servers stay reachable while connected: addresses like 192.168.x.x, 10.x.x.x and 172.16–31.x.x, and names without a dot or ending in .local or .lan. Everything else still goes through the VPN.

    To the profile used last, before any page loads.
