Ad blocking

Requests the ad blocker stopped on the page, newest first, with the filter that matched and the list it comes from. Addresses are shown without their query. This list lives only in memory and starts again when the page navigates. Elements hidden by cosmetic filters are not listed.

Ad blocking is turned off (Settings → Privacy). Changes here apply when it is on.

Lists are checked for updates once a day. A list you add must be an https:// address of a text file in Adblock Plus or uBlock Origin syntax (at most 16 MB); if it cannot be downloaded it is not added. “Restore default lists” turns the built-in lists back to their usual choice; lists you added and My filters stay.

Your own filters, in Adblock Plus or uBlock Origin syntax: ||host^ blocks a host, site##.selector hides elements on a site, @@ makes an exception. Lines starting with ! are comments. They take effect as soon as they are saved, without a restart.
