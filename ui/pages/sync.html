Sync your browsing on all your devices

Bookmarks, history, settings, pins and lists — and your passwords if you want — are encrypted on this device before they leave it. Only your devices can read them: the Krunk server stores encrypted data it can't read.

Nothing is sent for sync until you turn it on.

Save your passphrase and recovery key

These two keep your synced data private. With either one, you can restore your data on a new device if you lose all the others. Write them down or save them somewhere safe now: they are shown only once.
