Values are little-endian, as WebAssembly stores them. A frozen value is written back about 20 times a second by the page's own timers (slower while its tab is in the background). Scans, freezes and this list live in memory only: they end with the tab. Nothing here hides the inspection from the page. A memory that grows (memory.grow) keeps its number, its scans, its watch entries and its frozen values. A global's value is written when you press Enter; mutable globals only.
