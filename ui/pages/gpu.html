Media codecs

This engine build decodes VP8, VP9, AV1, Opus, Vorbis, FLAC and MP3. It has no H.264, HEVC, AAC, AC-3 or E-AC-3 decoders, so Plex direct play and transcodes, and other H.264/AAC-only streams, do not play. A codec-enabled engine needs a separate build (see the README).
